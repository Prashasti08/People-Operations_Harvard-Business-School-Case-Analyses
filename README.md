# People Operations | From Root Causes to Action

**Harvard Business School Case Analyses**

I analyzed 3 workforce challenges to identify what was driving them and recommended evidence-based actions. The cases span **talent acquisition, employee retention, and change management**.

| Company | Company type | Analysis & recommendations | Full analysis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SG Cowen** | Investment banking | **Analysis:** Evaluated four recruiting stages and compared finalists. <br>• Add job-relevant assessments and involve a talent acquisition specialist. <br>• Set a deadline for final hiring decisions. | [View PDF](sg-cowen-recruitment-analysis.pdf) |
| **Eastern Massachusetts University Hospital** | Healthcare | **Analysis:** Identified staffing shortages, burnout, and inconsistent feedback as risks to nurse retention. <br>• Improve staffing workflows and standardize performance feedback. <br>• Expand training for junior staff. | [View PDF](barbara-norris-change-analysis.pdf) |
| **KANA** | CRM software | **Analysis:** Conducted a fishbone root-cause analysis after news of a planned layoff leaked. <br>• Have senior leaders communicate the decision and coach managers for follow-up conversations. <br>• Offer career-transition support to affected employees. | [View PDF](kana-restructuring-analysis.pdf) |
