# People Operations  From Root Causes to Action

**Harvard Business School case analyses by Prashasti Tripathi**

I analyzed three workforce challenges and recommended evidence-based strategies for **talent acquisition, employee retention, and change management**.

| Organization Name | Organization Type | Analysis & Recommendations | Full Analysis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SG Cowen** | Investment bank | **Analysis:** Evaluated four recruiting stages and compared finalists.<br>• Add job-relevant assessments and involve a talent acquisition specialist.<br>• Set deadlines for final hiring decisions. | [View PDF](sg-cowen-recruitment-analysis.pdf) |
| **Eastern Massachusetts University Hospital** | Academic hospital | **Analysis:** Identified staffing shortages, burnout, and inconsistent feedback as retention risks.<br>• Improve staffing workflows and standardize performance feedback.<br>• Expand training for junior nurses. | [View PDF](barbara-norris-change-analysis.pdf) |
| **KANA** | CRM software company | **Analysis:** Used fishbone analysis to identify gaps in layoff planning and communication.<br>• Have senior leaders communicate the decision and prepare managers for follow-up conversations.<br>• Offer career-transition support to affected employees. | [View PDF](kana-restructuring-analysis.pdf) |
