# People Operations | Harvard Business School Case Analyses

Root-cause analysis and evidence-based recommendations for hiring, retention, and workforce change

| Organization Name | Organization Type | Challenge Analyzed | Actionable Recommendations | Full Analysis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SG Cowen** | Investment bank | Evaluated **four stages of the recruitment funnel** and compared finalists. Identified inconsistent screening and delays in hiring decisions. | • Introduce job-relevant skills assessments.<br>• Involve a talent acquisition specialist throughout the process.<br>• Set deadlines for final hiring decisions. | [View PDF](sg-cowen-recruitment-analysis.pdf) |
| **Eastern Massachusetts University Hospital** | Academic hospital | Identified **staffing shortages, burnout, and inconsistent performance feedback** as risks to nurse retention. | • Improve staffing workflows to relieve workload pressure.<br>• Standardize performance criteria and feedback.<br>• Expand training for junior nurses. | [View PDF](barbara-norris-change-analysis.pdf) |
| **KANA** | CRM software company | Conducted a **fishbone root-cause analysis** after news of a planned layoff leaked. Identified gaps in leadership communication, manager readiness, and employee support. | • Assign senior leaders ownership of layoff communications.<br>• Coach managers on employee conversations.<br>• Provide outplacement and career-transition support. | [View PDF](kana-restructuring-analysis.pdf) |
