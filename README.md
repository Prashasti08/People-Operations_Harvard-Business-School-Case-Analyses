# People Operations | Harvard Business School Case Analyses

Root-cause analysis and evidence-based recommendations for challenges regarding Talent Acquisition, Employee Retention, and Workforce Restructuring

| Organization Name | Organization Type | Challenge Analyzed | Actionable Recommendations | Full Analysis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SG Cowen** | Investment bank | Evaluated **4 stages of the recruitment funnel** and compared finalists. Identified inconsistent screening and delays in hiring decisions. | • Involve a talent acquisition specialist from campus sourcing through final selection.<br>• Add numerical reasoning and financial risk assessments during screening.<br>• Set a deadline for hiring decisions after final interviews. | [View PDF](SGCowinCase_Prashasti_Tripathi.-2.pdf) |
| **Eastern Massachusetts University Hospital** | Academic hospital | Identified **staffing shortages, burnout, and inconsistent performance feedback** as risks to nurse retention. | • Improve shift coverage to reduce reliance on float staff.<br>• Define performance criteria and schedule regular feedback check-ins.<br>• Expand training for junior nurses and patient care assistants. | [View PDF](NorrisCase_Prashasti_Tripathi.pdf) |
| **KANA** | CRM software company | Conducted a **fishbone root-cause analysis** after news of a planned layoff leaked. Identified gaps in leadership communication, manager readiness, and employee support. | • Have senior leaders issue the layoff announcement and set a schedule for updates.<br>• Coach managers on delivering layoff news and answering employee questions.<br>• Offer résumé workshops and job-search support to affected employees. | [View PDF](Kana%20case_Prashasti_Tripathi-1.pdf) |
