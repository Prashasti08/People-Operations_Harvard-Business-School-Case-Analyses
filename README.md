# People Operations | Harvard Business School Case Analyses

 Root-cause analysis and evidence-based recommendations for **talent acquisition, employee retention, and workforce restructuring**.

| **Organization Name** | **Organization Type** | **Challenges Analyzed** | **Actionable Recommendations** | **Case Materials** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SG Cowen** | Investment Bank | • Bottlenecks across **4 recruiting stages**<br>• Inconsistent screening and selection bias risk<br>• Slow final decisions and finalist drop-off risk | • Embed a talent acquisition specialist across all 4 stages<br>• Add numerical reasoning and financial risk assessments<br>• Set a post-interview decision deadline | [Case Analysis PDF](SGCowinCase_Prashasti_Tripathi.-2.pdf) |
| **Eastern Massachusetts University Hospital** | Academic Hospital | • Staffing gaps and nurse burnout<br>• Inconsistent performance feedback<br>• Low engagement and retention risk | • Improve shift coverage and reduce reliance on float staff<br>• Standardize performance criteria and feedback check-ins<br>• Train junior nurses and patient care assistants | [Case Analysis PDF](NorrisCase_Prashasti_Tripathi.pdf)<br>[Action Plan for Norris PDF](Action%20Plan%20for%20Norris.pdf) |
| **KANA** | B2B CRM Software Company | • Premature disclosure of layoffs<br>• Gaps in executive communication and manager readiness<br>• Limited employee transition support | • Assign executives ownership of layoff communications<br>• Coach managers on layoff conversations<br>• Provide outplacement and job-search support | [Case Analysis PDF](Kana%20case_Prashasti_Tripathi-1.pdf) |
